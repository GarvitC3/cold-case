## Winter roads in northern Ontario (cited)
* Ontario funds about **3,200 km of winter roads connecting 32 remote First Nations** (Ministry of Northern Development spokesperson, as reported by CBC).
  [CBC: Climate change threatens winter roads connecting northern Ontario's remote communities](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.6993030)
* Nishnawbe Aski Nation (NAN) says climate change has **shortened the average winter-road season from 77 days to as few as 28 days in some areas**.
  [CTV Northern Ontario: Impassable winter roads create 'dire' situation](https://northernontario.ctvnews.ca/impassable-winter-roads-create-dire-situation-for-ontario-first-nations-nan-1.6763990)
* During the unusually warm 2023–24 winter, NAN declared an emergency; Temagami First Nation's road closed 1 March, described by its leaders as likely its shortest season on record.
  [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10287501/winter-roads-dire-situation-ontario-first-nations/amp) · [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10380771/ontario-ice-road-first-nation/)
* Research: Jien & Tsuji (2018), *Climatic Change* 148 — projected climate-change impacts on Ontario's Far North winter-road systems.
  [RePEc listing](https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/climat/v148y2018i1d10.1007_s10584-018-2178-2.html)
